*Agents' Last Exam* (ALE) is a benchmark designed to evaluate AI agents on long-horizon, economically valuable, real-world tasks with verifiable outcomes. Developed in collaboration with 250+ industry experts, it covers non-physical industries defined with reference to the O\*NET / SOC 2018 occupational taxonomy, organised around a task taxonomy of 55 sub-fields in 13 industry clusters spanning 1K+ tasks. Across mainstream harness and backbone configurations, the average full pass rate on the hardest tier remains below 1%.

**My role**: data contributor — one of the community contributors who authored and validated tasks for the benchmark. Led by Yiyou Sun, Xinyang Han, Weichen Zhang et al., advised by Dawn Song.
